## Songs (the Kettle Whistle)

Notes are played with the item slots and the action button, five notes: **A ▲ ◀ ▶ ▼**
(keyboard: Space, I, U, O, K).

| Song | Taught by | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fennel's Tune | Fennel (Thicket) | talk to Fennel from anywhere; make Biscotti dance |
| Saffron's Lullaby | Nanny Kimchi | opens royal seals, wakes Lord Gulp |
| Custard's Song | Crème | calls Custard, pleases cows (milk) |
| Simmer Song | the royal tomb | skip to dawn or dusk; freezes Stalebies |
| Song of Rising | Old Mozz | moves blue time blocks; switches era at the Cleaver's block |
| Boiling Song | windmill man | brings rain and storms; drains the well |
| Minuet of Thyme, Bolero of Embers, Serenade of Brine, Nocturne of Rot, Requiem of Sugar, Prelude of the Hearth | Cinder | warp to each temple / the Temple of the First Hearth |

## Quest items

- **The three Embers** (Courage, Power, Wisdom) — the Fresh-era quest.
- **Six Keeper medallions** (Umami, Bitter, Spice, Salt, Sour, Sweet) — the Risen-era quest.
- **Dungeon items:** Map, Compass, small keys, Big Key (each dungeon).
- **Golden Weevil tokens** (100): rewards at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100.
- **Fizzy's Letter**, **Saffron's Letter**, the **Royal Recipe** — story items.
