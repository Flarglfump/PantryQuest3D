# Pantry Quest 3D — Theme, Story, Characters, Setting

> *"Time can ripen, or time can ruin. A good cook knows the difference."* — Old Mozz

Pantry Quest 3D is the third Pantry Quest game and the series' first in 3D: a
third-person action adventure in the shape of the late-90s console epics — a big
open field joining towns and dungeons, a lock-on fairy companion, an instrument that
plays magic tunes, and a story told across two eras of one hero's life.

## Theme

**Time ripens or ruins.** Everything in the Pantry Kingdom changes with time. Bread
rises, cheese ages, cabbage ferments into kimchi, wine deepens — good cooks use time.
But left alone and unloved, food goes bad. The series' villains have always been
food "left too long" (Pantry Quest II's Count Fromage was a cheese forgotten in a
cellar for a century). In Pantry Quest 3D we finally meet the thing that whispered to
him: **the Mould**, the spirit of rot itself, now walking the world as **Duke Mildew**.

The game's big mechanic carries the theme. Sprig, the hero, is too young to wield the
legendary Cleaver of Dawn, so he is sealed in the **Proofing Vault** to *rise* — like
dough — for seven years. He wakes as a young adult into a kingdom that Mildew has left
to spoil. Moving between the **Fresh** era (child) and the **Risen** era (adult) is
how Sprig solves the world: a seed planted as a child is a vine to climb as an adult;
a cheese wheel set aside as a child has aged into a key.

Tone: warm, funny, a little melancholy. Food puns are the series' house style, but the
emotional core is sincere — forgotten things, growing up, the people who wait for you.

## Setting: the Pantry Kingdom

A land of kitchens and gardens the size of countries. Its regions are named for
pantry shelves and cooking traditions:

| Region | Inspired by | Folk |
|---|---|---|
| **Sprouthollow** | a hidden herb-garden village in a sunken forest | the Sprouts: leafy children who never grow up, each with a pilot-light spirit |
| **Breadbasket Fields** | the open field at the heart of it all | travellers, scarecrows, a crust-owl |
| **Larder Town & Castle Larder** | the royal capital | townsfolk, guards, the royal family |
| **Buttercup Dairy** | a farm on a hill | Farmer Marge, her daughter Crème, Custard the pudding-pony |
| **Peppermill Village** | a windmill town under the mountain | millers, the Weevil family, Nanny Kimchi |
| **Mount Fondue** | a volcano of bubbling cheese | the Hardtack Clan: biscuit-rock folk who eat stones |
| **Fizzwater Falls & Lake Brine** | soda springs and a great salt lake | the Fizzlings: bubbly soda folk |
| **The Thicket of Thyme** | a lost-woods maze of herbs | Skulk-scrubs, lost travellers |
| **The Sugar Dunes** | a desert of sugar and salt flats | the Paprika Raiders, a clan of spice bandits |
| **The Rotlands** | the far edge of the map, where food goes to spoil | Mildew's mould-born |

**The First Hearth.** Before the kingdom, the gods of cooking lit a fire and left three
**Embers** in it — *Courage* (Yeast: the ember that makes things rise), *Wisdom* (Salt:
the ember that preserves) and *Power* (Spice: the ember that transforms). Together they
are the **Hearthflame**: whoever holds it can cook the world into whatever they wish.
The Hearthflame sleeps in the **Pantry of Ages**, behind the Door of Seasons in the
Temple of the First Hearth, in Larder Town.

## Story

### Prologue — The Fresh Era

1. **Sprouthollow.** Every Sprout has a *pilot light*, a little flame spirit, except
   Sprig. He has nightmares of a castle in a storm and a pale figure in a mouldy cloak.
   One morning **Mother Starter** — the ancient sourdough who guards the village from her
   great crock — sends a pilot light of her own, **Flicker**, to wake him. "Mother Starter
   wants you. Now!"
2. Sprig needs a **Wooden Spoon** and a **Pot-Lid Shield** before **Chive**, the bossy
   Sprout who guards Mother Starter's path, will let him by. His best friend **Fennel**
   cheers him on and gives him a reed pipe tune.
3. **The Great Crock.** Mother Starter is sick: a mould has got into her crock and laid a
   queen mite there. Sprig goes *inside* her (dungeon 1), finds the **Pea Shooter**, and
   destroys **Queen Mildewmite**. But it is too late. With her last bubbles Mother Starter
   tells him the truth: he is no Sprout, but a human child she was given as a baby during
   a war. A **Duke from the Rotlands** cursed her for refusing to give him the **Ember of
   Courage** she kept. She gives it to Sprig and tells him to go to the castle, to the
   princess. Mother Starter falls silent. Fennel gives Sprig her pipe as he leaves; Chive,
   gruffly, says sorry.
4. **Breadbasket Fields, Castle Larder.** A crust-owl, **Sir Hootenanny**, guides Sprig to
   Larder Town. Sprig sneaks into the castle garden and meets **Princess Saffron**, who
   has dreamed the same dream. The pale man is **Duke Mildew**, now bowing to her father
   the king. He wants the Hearthflame; the Door of Seasons opens only with the three
   Embers and the **Kettle Whistle**, the royal family's treasure. Saffron asks Sprig to
   gather the other two Embers first. Her nanny, **Nanny Kimchi**, teaches him
   **Saffron's Lullaby** and escorts him out.
5. **The Ember of Power** is held by the Hardtack Clan on Mount Fondue. Their chief, **Big
   Biscotti**, sulks because Mildew blocked their food cave, Hardtack Caverns. Sprig plays
   Fennel's tune, Biscotti dances, gives him the Gum Bracelet; Sprig clears the caverns
   (dungeon 2, **Popcorn Bombs**, boss **King Crustodon**). Biscotti makes Sprig a
   "Sworn Brother" and gives the Ember of Power.
6. **The Ember of Wisdom** belongs to the Fizzlings. Their princess, **Effervescia
   ("Fizzy")**, has gone missing — inside **Lord Gulp**, the lake's giant guardian
   sturgeon, who has swallowed something nasty (dungeon 3, **Spatula Boomerang**, boss
   **the Gristle Knot**). Fizzy, rescued, gives him the Ember of Wisdom — "and that means
   we're engaged now, by the way." (A running joke paid off in the Risen era.)
7. **The betrayal.** Sprig returns to Larder Town in a storm. The castle is burning. Saffron
   and Nanny Kimchi flee on a horse past him; Saffron throws the **Kettle Whistle** into the
   moat. Mildew rides up behind them, sneering. Sprig dives for the whistle, hears Saffron's
   voice in a vision, and opens the Door of Seasons with the three Embers and the whistle.
   Inside rests the **Cleaver of Dawn** in its butcher's block. He pulls it — and Mildew,
   who followed him, walks past him into the Pantry of Ages. "Thank you, little sprout."

### The Risen Era — seven years later

8. **The Proofing Vault.** Sprig wakes in a room of warm stone. **Old Mozz** — the cheese
   butler of Pantry Quest II, now the Keeper of Umami — explains: the Cleaver chose him, but
   he was too small to carry it, so the vault sealed him away to rise for seven years.
   Mildew used the open door to seize the Hearthflame — but it split. He holds only the
   **Ember of Power**. Courage stayed with Sprig. Wisdom went to someone unknown.
   To stop Mildew, Sprig must wake the **five other Keepers of Flavour** in five temples.
9. **Cinder.** On the steps outside waits a masked stranger in grey, **Cinder**, who says
   they are "the last of the Sootfolk" and teaches warp songs at each temple. (Cinder is
   Saffron in disguise, holding the Ember of Wisdom. Revealed in the finale.)
10. **The five temples**, each waking a Keeper who was a friend in the Fresh era:
    - **Thyme Temple** (Thicket of Thyme): **Fennel**, Keeper of Bitter (herbs).
      Boss: **the Hollow Duke**, a mould puppet of Mildew riding out of paintings.
    - **Fondue Forge** (Mount Fondue): **Big Biscotti**, Keeper of Spice.
      Boss: **Gratinox**, the broiling dragon Mildew woke under the mountain.
    - **Fizzwater Temple** (Lake Brine, frozen over by Mildew's cold rot): **Fizzy**, Keeper
      of Salt. Boss: **the Living Brine**.
    - **Compost Crypt** (under Peppermill's graveyard): **Nanny Kimchi**, Keeper of Sour.
      Boss: **the Maw of Rot**, invisible but for its hands.
    - **Salt Idol Temple** (Sugar Dunes; played as both child *and* adult):
      **Madame Caramel**, the Paprika Raiders' rebel second-in-command, Keeper of Sweet.
      Bosses: **Saccharine and Sodium**, the twin witches who raised Mildew.
11. **The Revelation.** With all six Keepers awake, Cinder unmasks as **Saffron**: she holds
    the Ember of Wisdom. Mildew, sensing it, takes her in a crystal of mould.
12. **The Moldering Spire.** Mildew's tower grows from the ruin of Castle Larder like a
    mushroom. The Keepers bridge the rot-moat with a rainbow of six flavours. Sprig climbs
    through six trials (one per Keeper), frees Saffron, and fights **Duke Mildew** at the
    top. The spire collapses (escape sequence, as in Pantry Quest II). In the ruins Mildew,
    swollen by the Ember of Power, becomes **Gorgemold**, the Rotting Beast — the shape
    every forgotten thing could become. Saffron holds it with Light, Sprig strikes with the
    Cleaver, and the Keepers seal it in the compost of the Void.
13. **Ending.** Saffron uses the Kettle Whistle to send Sprig back to the Fresh era, to live
    the years he lost. The final scene: young Sprig in the castle garden, meeting Saffron
    for the first time again — and, back in Sprouthollow, a tiny green bubble in Mother
    Starter's crock. She is starting again.

## Characters

### Heroes

- **Sprig** — the hero. A human boy raised as a Sprout, so he wears a leafy cap and a
  green apron. Brave, quiet, kind. (Fresh: about 10. Risen: about 17.) Silent
  protagonist; he answers with choices.
- **Flicker** — Sprig's pilot light, a bossy little blue flame. Locks on to enemies
  ("Focus"), notices things ("Hey! Look!"), and gives hints on C-up/`Tab`. Shy about the
  fact that she is scared of water.
- **Princess Saffron** — the king's daughter. Clever, dreamy, stubborn. Sees the future in
  dreams. Disguised as **Cinder** in the Risen era.

### Keepers of Flavour (the "sages")

| Keeper | Flavour | Who they are | Temple |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Old Mozz** | Umami | the old cheese butler from Pantry Quest II, now guardian of the First Hearth | (Temple of the First Hearth) |
| **Fennel** | Bitter | Sprig's best friend in Sprouthollow; plays a reed pipe | Thyme Temple |
| **Big Biscotti** | Spice | the loud, generous chief of the Hardtack Clan | Fondue Forge |
| **Princess Effervescia** | Salt | the Fizzling princess; vain, brave, decides she's Sprig's fiancée | Fizzwater Temple |
| **Nanny Kimchi** | Sour | Saffron's fierce old nurse, of the secret Pickler order | Compost Crypt |
| **Madame Caramel** | Sweet | rebel of the Paprika Raiders who wants them free of Mildew | Salt Idol Temple |

### Villains

- **Duke Mildew** — the Mould, given a body. A tall, elegant figure in a cloak of grey-green
  fuzz, with a voice like a cellar. He is *patient*: he believes everything rots in the end,
  so he is only helping it along. (He was the whisper in Count Fromage's cellar.)
- **Saccharine and Sodium** — twin witches of the Sugar Dunes, one sweet-hot, one salt-cold,
  who raised Mildew as their "little culture". Bicker constantly.
- **The Hollow Duke** — Mildew's mould puppet, riding out of portraits.
- **The mould-born** — Mildew's spoiled creatures: mildewmites, fuzzballs, spore-bats,
  sourdough zombies ("Stalebies"), rot-knights.

### Supporting cast

- **Mother Starter** — the ancient sourdough in a great crock, guardian of Sprouthollow.
- **Chive** — bossy Sprout boy; blocks the path, later apologises.
- **The Sprout twins (Mint and Dill)**, **Sage the shopkeeper**, **Know-It-All Parsley** (the
  tutorial signs come from him).
- **Sir Hootenanny** — a crust-owl who appears at crossroads with long-winded advice
  ("Did you get all that? Shall I say it again?").
- **Farmer Marge, Crème, Custard** — Buttercup Dairy. Crème teaches **Custard's Song**.
  Custard is the pudding-pony Sprig rides in the Risen era. **Grubb**, the farmhand, takes
  over the dairy for Mildew while Marge is away.
- **King Larder XIV** — Saffron's father; vague, fond of soup.
- **The Weevil family** — cursed into golden weevils by greed; collect **Golden Weevils** to
  turn them back (100 in the world).
- **Grandpa Gristle** — the gravekeeper of Peppermill; runs a grave-digging game.
- **Biggrumble** — an enormous Hardtack on the summit, who forges the Big Chef's Knife.
- **The Mask Maker** in Larder Town; **the Bean Merchant**; **the Fishing Pond Man**.

## Ties to the earlier games

- **Pip** (Pantry Quest's hero) is the royal head chef in Larder Town; he appears in both eras,
  older, and gives Sprig the Copper Pan. **Basil** (Pantry Quest II) runs a travelling
  kitchen cart in Breadbasket Fields and sells Heart Pie slices and bottled sauce.
- **Old Mozz** returns as the Keeper of Umami.
- **Count Fromage's diary** pages appear as lore in the Compost Crypt: the last line,
  "It is very good at whispering", is answered when Mildew says the same words to Sprig.
- **Heart Pies** and **beans** return from Pantry Quest as health and money.
